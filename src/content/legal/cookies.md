Cookies are small text files a website stores on your device. Similar technologies, such as your browser's local storage, work in much the same way. This policy explains which ones exploreans.com uses.

## Your choice

When you first visit, we ask whether you accept analytics cookies. Until you accept, none are set. You can change your mind at any time with the **Cookie settings** link at the foot of every page.

## What we use

**Strictly necessary.** These remember your cookie choice, so we don't ask you on every page. The site needs them to work, so they don't need your consent.

| Name | Provider | Purpose | Kept for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| exploreans-consent | Exploreans (local storage) | Remembers whether you accepted or declined analytics cookies | 12 months |

**Analytics.** These tell us how visitors use the site, such as which pages are read most and how people arrive, so we can improve it. They are set only if you accept.

| Name | Provider | Purpose | Kept for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| _ga | Google Analytics | Tells one visitor apart from another | 2 years |
| _ga_RX50FM3PQE | Google Analytics | Keeps track of your current visit | 2 years |

We do not use advertising or social media cookies on this website.

## Other websites

Links to our places take you to their own websites, such as mara.exploreans.com and watamu.exploreans.com. Those sites have their own cookie and privacy policies.

## Managing cookies in your browser

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Most browsers explain how in their help pages. The site works fully without analytics cookies.

## Contact

If you have questions about cookies on this site, write to [privacy@planhotel.com](mailto:privacy@planhotel.com). Our [Privacy Policy](/privacy/) explains how we handle personal data.
